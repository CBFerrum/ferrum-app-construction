# Provenance

This app's design and code are its own. No code was copied from another project.

- Its shape follows common practice for building contracts: a schedule of values, variations
  (change orders) priced and approved, progress claims (payment applications) by percentage done,
  and retention held until completion.
- The invoices are ERP's Sales Invoices, made through the platform's records as Property,
  Hospitality and Education make theirs.
- No construction reference was copied into `ERP/referance` for it: ERPNext has no such module, and
  the candidates we know of are under licences this project does not copy from.
